# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, intimidated, or unsafe. Examples include unwanted physical contact, sexual comments or jokes, inappropriate messages, repeated unwanted advances, showing explicit content, or asking for sexual favours. Such behaviour can occur in person, online, or through any form of communication.

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

If I face or witness such behaviour, I would not ignore it. I would document the incident, preserve any evidence if available, and report it to the appropriate authority such as my manager, HR, or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). If someone else is affected, I would support them and encourage them to report the incident. Maintaining a safe and respectful workplace is everyone's responsibility.
