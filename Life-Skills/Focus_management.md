# Focus Management

### Question 1
Deep Work is the ability to focus without distraction on mentally demanding tasks. 
It means working with complete concentration for a dedicated period of time to produce high-quality results, learn faster, and create more value compared to distracted work.

---

### Question 2
According to the author, deep work can be done properly by:

- Eliminating distractions like social media and unnecessary notifications  
- Training concentration like a skill through regular practice  
- Creating routines and environments that support focused work  
- Setting clear goals before starting a session  
- Prioritizing important work over busy work  
- Taking breaks to maintain mental energy  

---

### Question 3
I can (and will try to) implement deep work principles in my day-to-day life by:

- Keeping phone notifications turned off during work/study sessions  
- Working on one task at a time instead of multitasking  
- Setting small deadlines for assignments and learning goals  
- Taking intentional breaks between focused sessions  
- Spending less time on social media and more time on meaningful work  

---

### Question 4
Some dangers of social media are:

- Constant distraction and reduced attention span  
- Lower ability to focus deeply on important work  
- Addiction to instant gratification and quick rewards  
- Increased comparison with others and reduced satisfaction  
- Wasting time without realizing it  
- Information overload and mental fatigue
