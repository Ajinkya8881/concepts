# Focus Management

## Question 1

Deep Work refers to the practice of giving complete attention to tasks that require thinking, learning, and problem-solving without interruptions. It involves working with intense focus for a specific period so that the quality of output improves and tasks are completed more effectively. This type of work also helps build stronger skills and deeper understanding.

---

## Question 2

The author suggests that developing deep work requires intentional habits and discipline. Some important approaches include:

* Reducing interruptions from phones, notifications, and unnecessary online activity
* Practicing concentration regularly so focus becomes stronger over time
* Designing a workspace and routine that encourages productive work
* Defining clear objectives before beginning a work session
* Spending more time on meaningful tasks instead of staying busy without progress
* Taking planned breaks to maintain energy and avoid burnout

---

## Question 3

I plan to apply deep work principles in my daily routine through the following actions:

* Keep distracting applications and notifications disabled while studying or working
* Focus on completing one important task before moving to another
* Break larger goals into smaller sessions with dedicated time blocks
* Schedule short recovery breaks between periods of focused effort
* Limit unnecessary screen time and spend more time on learning and practical work
* Create a habit of working with full attention instead of switching constantly between tasks

## Question 4

Some negative effects of excessive social media usage are:

* Reduced ability to maintain long periods of concentration
* Frequent interruptions that affect productivity
* Dependence on instant feedback and short-term rewards
* Increased comparison that may affect confidence and satisfaction
* Loss of valuable time without noticing
* Mental overload caused by consuming too much information continuously
* Difficulty staying present and engaged in important activities
